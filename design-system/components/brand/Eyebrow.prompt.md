One-sentence: the mono uppercase kicker that labels a section above its serif heading.

```jsx
<Eyebrow>Portfólio em exclusivo</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow tone="light">Real Estate · Construction · Interiors</Eyebrow>   // on navy/graphite
<Eyebrow tone="inverse">Paleta</Eyebrow>
```

Always mono, always uppercase, always above the heading with 14–28px of air.
